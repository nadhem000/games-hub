How to Play

  • Click a piece to select it, then click a valid square to move.
  • Pawns promote automatically to Queen.
  • The AI opponent uses Stockfish (or a fallback engine).
  • Select difficulty before starting a new game.
  • Use the Hint button to see a suggested move.
